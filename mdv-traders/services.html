Recommended replacement intervals (general guide)

  • Sediment & carbon filters: every 6 to 12 months
  • RO membrane: every 2 to 3 years
  • UV lamp: about once a year
This is only a general guide. Actual life depends on your water quality (TDS), daily usage and the manufacturer's advice. Our technician will check and tell you what really needs replacing.
